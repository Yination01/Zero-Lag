## What Zero-Lag is

Zero-Lag is an Android utility for mobile gamers. It measures network
quality (ping, jitter, packet loss), shows signal and device performance
readings, and guides you to Android settings.

## What it does not do

Zero-Lag cannot increase cellular tower signal, overclock your phone, close
other apps for you, or change another app's settings. All readings are
estimates from local measurements.
