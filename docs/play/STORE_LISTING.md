## Short description

> Measure public-edge latency, track readiness, and monitor a gaming HUD.

This is within the usual 80-character short-description limit. Do not replace
it with claims that Zero-Lag reduces game-server ping, raises mobile signal,
boosts tower strength, overclocks a device, or silently closes apps.

## Full description

> Zero-Lag helps mobile gamers understand phone and network readiness before a
> match. Run a local readiness check to see a public-edge latency estimate,
> jitter, failed web probes, and a clear readiness verdict.
>
> With your permission, Zero-Lag can show a small floating HUD while you play.
> The HUD shows a live public-edge estimate and device memory information, with
> an ongoing Android notification that lets you stop it.
>
> Optional Usage Access helps Zero-Lag identify a supported game in the
> foreground. Optional location and phone-state access help label signal and
> network conditions. You choose whether to grant each permission, and the app
> remains usable when optional access is denied.
>
> Readiness history stays on your device in this version. Zero-Lag has no
> account requirement, advertising SDK, cloud history, or Zero-Lag server.
> Clear History removes stored readiness results from the app.
>
> Zero-Lag does not measure exact game-server ping or in-game packet loss. It
> cannot raise tower signal, overclock your device, change a game's graphics,
> or silently close other apps. Guided actions open Android settings that you
> control.
